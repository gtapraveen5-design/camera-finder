Camera Finder

Each tab catches a different kind of hidden camera. Use all five for a full room sweep.

Finds cameras connected to the Wi-Fi you're on. Connect to the room's Wi-Fi in Settings first.

Finds cameras broadcasting their own Wi-Fi hotspot, or hidden behind a portable 4G router — the ones the network scan can't see. You don't need to connect to anything.

Tip: to find where a flagged hotspot is, walk around the room and scan again. The signal gets stronger (closer to 0 dBm) as you get closer; stronger than −50 dBm usually means within a few metres. Android limits fresh scans to about 4 every 2 minutes.

Walk toward a flagged hotspot and the phone beeps faster as you get closer, like a metal detector. Pick a target from the Hotspots tab first.

Finds cameras that are offline, asleep or recording to an SD card — they send nothing over Wi-Fi, but their lens still reflects light.

Lens finder

Flashlight + red contrast filter. Circles small, steady bright dots.
  1. Turn the room lights off and close the curtains.
  2. Hold the phone at eye level and sweep slowly, one wall at a time.
  3. A lens shows as a tiny dot that stays put as you move. Red circle = steady.
  4. Walk up to anything circled and look closely for a pinhole.

Infrared (night-vision) check

Night-vision cameras light the room with invisible IR LEDs. Many phone cameras can see them.
  1. Test first: point a TV remote at the phone and press a button. If the remote's LED flashes on screen, this camera sees IR.
  2. In the dark, scan the room. Look for small purple/white glowing dots.
  3. If the remote test fails, tap Flip camera — selfie cameras often see IR better.

Finds powered electronics hidden inside objects, even with no network at all, using the phone's compass sensor.

  1. Hold the phone in the open, away from objects, and tap Start (or Re-calibrate).
  2. Slowly pass the top of the phone 2–5 cm over each object. It beeps faster as the reading rises.
  3. A jump on something that shouldn't contain electronics — a picture frame, book, plant, tissue box, clock face, wall hook — is a red flag. Look closer.
Speakers, magnets, metal and in-wall wiring also move the reading, so compare similar objects and use it together with the lens finder.
