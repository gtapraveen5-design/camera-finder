Camera Finder

See every device on your Wi-Fi and flag the ones that look like cameras.

This tool only observes your own network — it lists devices, reads the hardware maker from the MAC address, and checks for camera video ports. It never logs into or sends commands to any device. A flag means “worth checking,” not proof; confirm anything suspicious physically (look for a lens, use a flashlight to catch reflections, or unplug/cover it).
Defensive / self-protection use only. Run it only on a network you are using. MAC-vendor matching is best-effort and incomplete; an open RTSP port (554) is the most reliable camera signal. No data leaves your computer.
